# Vorlagen für den Verkauf / Templates for selling

Text blocks for the emails Srijana sends when someone asks about a painting.
Nothing here belongs on the website — these go in the message where she quotes
a price, because that is where the legal duty sits.

**Not legal advice.** These are drafted from the standard statutory wording
(the Muster-Widerrufsbelehrung in Anlage 1 zu Art. 246a § 1 Abs. 2 EGBGB).
Have a Rechtsanwalt or a Verbraucherzentrale check them once before the first
sale — it is cheap, and reusing them afterwards costs nothing.

---

## 1. Why this matters

Selling to a private person by email, phone or post is a **Fernabsatzvertrag**
(distance contract). Two things follow:

1. The buyer has **14 days** to withdraw, no reason needed.
2. She must give them the **Widerrufsbelehrung before** they buy — not after,
   not on request. If it is missing, the withdrawal period stretches to twelve
   months and fourteen days.

The simplest way to satisfy this is to put the text at the bottom of every
quote email. It costs one paste.

**Exception — commissioned work.** A painting made specifically to the
customer's order (a portrait of their family, a chosen size) is exempt under
§ 312g Abs. 2 Nr. 1 BGB. There is no right of withdrawal, but she should say so
explicitly in the quote, otherwise it is unclear.

**Business buyers** (a café, a practice, a company) are not consumers, so none
of this applies to them.

> Muster-Widerrufsformular verwenden, das jedoch nicht vorgeschrieben ist.
>
> Zur Wahrung der Widerrufsfrist reicht es aus, dass Sie die Mitteilung über die
> Ausübung des Widerrufsrechts vor Ablauf der Widerrufsfrist absenden.
>
> *Folgen des Widerrufs*
> Wenn Sie diesen Vertrag widerrufen, habe ich Ihnen alle Zahlungen, die ich von

> zurückzuzahlen, an dem die Mitteilung über Ihren Widerruf dieses Vertrags bei

>
> Sie haben die Ware unverzüglich und in jedem Fall spätestens binnen vierzehn
> Tagen ab dem Tag, an dem Sie mich über den Widerruf dieses Vertrags

## 3. Quote email — commissioned work (German)

Same structure, but the withdrawal block is **replaced** by:

> **Hinweis zum Widerrufsrecht**
> Es handelt sich um eine Auftragsarbeit, die nach Ihren individuellen Wünschen
> angefertigt wird. Bei solchen Waren besteht gemäß § 312g Abs. 2 Nr. 1 BGB kein
> Widerrufsrecht. Ich bespreche den Entwurf vor Beginn und während der Arbeit mit
> Ihnen, sodass Sie jederzeit sehen, wie das Bild entsteht.

Suggested commission terms to state in the same email:

> Anzahlung: {X} % bei Auftragserteilung, Restbetrag nach Fertigstellung
> Bearbeitungszeit: ca. {X} Wochen ab Erhalt der Vorlage
> Vorlage: ein gut belichtetes Foto in möglichst hoher Auflösung
> Änderungswünsche: {X} Korrekturschleifen inbegriffen

## 4. English version, for international enquiries

> **Right of withdrawal**
> As a consumer you have the right to withdraw from this contract within 14 days
> without giving any reason. The period begins on the day you, or a third party
> named by you who is not the carrier, take possession of the goods.
>
> To exercise it, inform me (Srijana Gurung Shrestha, {ADDRESS}, {PHONE},
> srijana.art.gallery@gmail.com) by a clear statement — a letter or an email is
> enough. Sending the notice before the period expires is sufficient to meet the
> deadline.
>
> If you withdraw, I will refund all payments received from you, including
> standard delivery costs, without undue delay and at the latest within 14 days
> of receiving your notice. I may withhold the refund until I have received the
> goods back or you have supplied proof of return, whichever is earlier. You must
> return the goods within 14 days of notifying me, and you bear the direct cost
> of return. You are only liable for any loss in value resulting from handling
> beyond what is necessary to establish the nature and characteristics of the
> item.
>
> This does not apply to commissioned work made to your specification.

## 5. Before the first shipped sale

- [ ] Register in the packaging register **LUCID** (`verpackungsregister.org`,
      free) and join a dual system. Required for sending packaged goods to
      consumers in Germany, including one-off sellers.
- [ ] Decide the standard shipping method and price so the quote can state it.
- [ ] Insure anything valuable — an unframed original in a cardboard tube is
      easily damaged, and the seller carries the risk until the consumer
      receives it.
- [ ] Keep a copy of every quote email. It is the contract, and the proof that
      the Widerrufsbelehrung was given in time.
